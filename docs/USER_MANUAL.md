## 💬 How to Use MediBot

### Step 1: Start a Chat

1. Click **"Start Chat"** or navigate to **Chat** page
2. You'll see a welcome message from MediBot
3. Type your symptoms in the text box

### Step 2: Describe Your Symptoms

**Be specific for better results:**

✅ **Good Examples:**
- "I have fever and headache since 2 days"
- "Severe chest pain and difficulty breathing"
- "I'm feeling nauseous and have stomach pain"
- "Mild cough with runny nose"

❌ **Vague Descriptions:**
- "I don't feel good"
- "Something is wrong"

### Step 3: Get Results

MediBot will analyze your symptoms and provide:

1. **Predicted Conditions** - Possible diseases with confidence scores
2. **Medical Advice** - What you should do
3. **Home Remedies** - Natural remedies you can try
4. **Doctor Alert** - If professional consultation is needed

### Step 4: Follow Recommendations

- **Mild conditions:** Follow home remedies and advice
- **Moderate conditions:** Monitor symptoms
- **Severe conditions:** Consult a doctor immediately

## 📊 Understanding Results

### Confidence Score

MediBot shows confidence percentage:

- **80-100%:** High confidence - Likely accurate
- **60-79%:** Moderate confidence - Possible match
- **Below 60%:** Low confidence - Consult doctor

### Severity Levels

**Mild:**
- Can be managed at home
- Over-the-counter medication may help
- Usually resolves on its own

**Moderate:**
- May need medical consultation
- Monitor symptoms closely
- Follow medical advice

**Severe:**
- Requires prompt medical attention
- Don't delay treatment
- Follow doctor's recommendations

**Critical:**
- **EMERGENCY** - Seek immediate help
- Call emergency services
- Go to nearest hospital

### Doctor Alert

When you see 🔴 **Doctor Alert**:

- MediBot detected serious symptoms
- Professional consultation recommended
- Don't ignore the alert
- Schedule appointment promptly

## ⚠️ Safety Guidelines

### Important Disclaimers

**MediBot is NOT:**
- ❌ A replacement for professional medical advice
- ❌ A diagnostic tool
- ❌ A substitute for doctor consultation
- ❌ An emergency service

**MediBot IS:**
- ✅ An AI-powered health information tool
- ✅ A symptom checker
- ✅ A health guidance assistant
- ✅ A health awareness platform

### When to See a Doctor Immediately

Seek emergency care if you experience:

- 🚨 **Chest pain** or pressure
- 🚨 **Difficulty breathing** or shortness of breath
- 🚨 **Severe bleeding** that won't stop
- 🚨 **Sudden severe pain** anywhere
- 🚨 **Confusion** or altered mental state
- 🚨 **Seizures** or convulsions
- 🚨 **Suicidal thoughts** or self-harm urges
- 🚨 **High fever** that doesn't respond to medication

### Privacy & Security

Your data is protected:

- ✅ Encrypted communication
- ✅ HIPAA-compliant storage
- ✅ No data sharing with third parties
- ✅ Secure authentication

## ❓ FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Is MediBot free to use?**
A: Yes, MediBot is completely free for basic usage.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: Yes, creating an account allows you to save consultation history.

**Q: Is my health data private?**
A: Yes, your data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your health information.

**Q: Can MediBot diagnose diseases?**
A: No, MediBot provides information and guidance, not diagnoses. Always consult a healthcare professional for diagnosis.

### Usage Questions

**Q: How accurate is MediBot?**
A: MediBot has high accuracy for common conditions, but accuracy varies. Always verify with a healthcare professional.

**Q: Can I use MediBot for children?**
A: MediBot is designed for adults. For children, always consult a pediatrician.

**Q: Does MediBot work offline?**
A: No, internet connection is required for MediBot to function.

**Q: Can I delete my consultation history?**
A: Yes, you can delete individual consultations from your dashboard.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**
A: Latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Currently, MediBot is web-based. Mobile app is coming soon.

**Q: What if MediBot doesn't understand my symptoms?**
A: Try describing symptoms more specifically. Use common medical terms.

## 🎯 Best Practices

### For Best Results:

1. ✅ **Be specific** about symptoms
2. ✅ **Include duration** (e.g., "since 2 days")
3. ✅ **Mention severity** (mild, moderate, severe)
4. ✅ **List all symptoms** you're experiencing
5. ✅ **Update your profile** with medical history
6. ✅ **Follow up** if symptoms persist

### Avoid:

1. ❌ Vague descriptions
2. ❌ Exaggerating symptoms
3. ❌ Ignoring doctor alerts
4. ❌ Self-medicating without advice
5. ❌ Delaying professional help
